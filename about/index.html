Our story

We got tired of watching good floors fail

We kept seeing epoxy floors lift within a year. The coating was almost never the problem. The prep was.

So we refuse to rush it:

  • Every floor mechanically ground — never acid-etched
  • Every crack chased and filled
  • Every slab moisture-checked before we quote it

When you hire us, you get us. Drayton and Dylan measure, run the grinder and answer the phone. We live here — we would rather earn the next job than close this one.

How we work

Four things we will not compromise on

  • Do it right the first timeNo shortcuts on prep, no thin coats. If the floor needs another step, it gets it.
  • Attention to detailClean edges, even flake, no roller marks. On a floor, the details are the job.
  • Hard work, no excusesWe show up when we said, hit the cure window, and clean up before we leave.
