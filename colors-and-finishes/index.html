Metallic pours

Metallic colors

Pigment moves through the resin as it levels, so the pattern is created live on your floor. Nobody can reproduce one exactly twice — including us.

Metallic swatches are rendered colour references, not photographs of installed floors. A metallic pour never looks the same twice and screens shift colour either way, so treat these as the palette rather than the exact result. We bring real sample boards to every estimate so you are choosing off the actual product.

Choosing

How to pick the right one

  • Match the mess, not the mood boardDarker blends and busier flake hide tire marks, salt and sawdust between cleanings. A near-white solid floor looks incredible on day one and shows every footprint on day two. Think about what actually lands on your floor before you pick.
  • Flake forgives, solid does notA solid color is a mirror: every trowel mark and repaired crack telegraphs through it in raking light. If your slab has history, flake breaks the surface up visually and the repairs disappear.
  • Look at it under your own lightsStorm Grey under a cool LED shop fixture and Storm Grey under a warm garage bulb are two different floors. This is exactly why we leave sample boards with you rather than asking you to decide off a screen.
  • Add grip where it gets wetFlake carries a little texture on its own. On a patio, a walkout basement or a garage that sees winter slush, we broadcast a fine non-slip aggregate into the topcoat — it costs very little and it changes how the floor behaves underfoot.

Color questions

Asked before every install

Can I get a custom blend?

Yes. The fifteen flake blends here are the ones we keep in stock, but chips come in dozens of colors and we mix to order — team colors, a match to your cabinets, or a tweak to one of ours. Bring a photo or a paint chip to the estimate and we will get close. Custom blends can add a few days of lead time.

Will the color fade?

Not under the topcoat we use. Bare epoxy ambers in sunlight, which is why every floor we install is sealed with a UV-stable polyaspartic or urethane clear. In a garage or basement it is a non-issue. On a patio or anywhere with direct sun, the topcoat is doing real work — and it is one of the reasons we do not skip it to save a few dollars.

How closely will my floor match the sample?

Flake matches closely, because the chips are a manufactured product and the blend is measured. Metallic is different by nature: the pigment moves as the resin levels, so your floor will share the sample's palette and character but the pattern will be its own. If you need predictability, flake is the safer pick.
