The radius

50+ miles from Champaign County

Roughly speaking: if you are within about a 50-mile drive of Champaign, we cover you, and we do not add a trip charge to get there.

That circle takes in all of Champaign County, McLean County and Piatt County, plus Decatur, Danville, Tuscola, Arcola, Paxton, Gibson City and a long list of smaller towns either side.

Close enough to come back

A contractor three hours away can sell you a floor, but they cannot easily swing by when you have a question about a spot near the overhead door two months later. We can, and we do.

Working close to home also means we schedule honestly. We are not stacking jobs across half the state and hoping the weather cooperates — if we tell you Tuesday, we mean Tuesday.
