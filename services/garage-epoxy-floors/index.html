What you get

A garage floor that shrugs off hot tires and road salt

A garage slab takes more abuse than any other floor in the house: hot tires parking on it, road salt melting off the fenders every February, oil drips, jack stands and whatever you dropped last weekend. Bare concrete soaks all of that up and never lets go. A properly installed epoxy floor seals it out, wipes clean with a mop, and makes the whole garage read as finished space instead of storage.

  • Diamond-ground surface prep, so the coating bonds instead of peeling in a year
  • Hot-tire resistant — no lifting or delamination where the car parks
  • Solid color, partial flake or full-broadcast flake, in a blend you pick
  • Most two- and three-car garages are a one- to two-day install

What it costs

Pricing for garage epoxy floors

Epoxy is priced by the square foot, and we will not quote a floor we have not seen — a number pulled over the phone helps nobody. Here is what actually moves it:

  • Square footage — a two-car garage and a four-car outbuilding price very differently per foot
  • Slab condition — cracking, pitting and spalling at the apron all add repair time
  • Finish — solid color, partial flake or full-broadcast flake
  • Extras — non-slip aggregate, cove base at the walls, and a second clear coat
