Choosing a system

Solid color, flake or metallic?

Three finishes, three different jobs. The room usually decides it for you.

Solid color

Cheapest, least forgiving — it shows every patch. Best on a clean modern pour.

Flake

Mid-price and right for most floors. Hides repairs, adds grip. Repaired slab? Pick this.

Metallic

The premium finish — depth like polished stone, no two alike. Needs a flatter slab and costs more.

Sometimes the answer is not to coat it at all — a failing slab isn’t a candidate, and we’ll say so at the estimate. Call (217) 372-7770 or request a free quote — we bring real samples and tell you straight.
